The paper describes PUDA (Physical Unified Device Architecture), a command-line runtime that lets AI agents observe, orient, decide, and act in the lab while hardware execution stays deterministic, atomic, and auditable.

Authors: Zekun Ren, Hongzhao Tan, JiaEn Yee, Kedar Hippalgaonkar.

For a walkthrough of the paper, see the [blog post](/blog/paper).
